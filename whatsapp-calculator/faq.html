The five questions everyone asks

The app on your phone is, and stays that way. What carries a price is the WhatsApp Business Platform, the official connection that a CRM, a helpdesk or an AI agent uses to send on your behalf. Meta charges it per delivered message, from a fraction of a cent to a few cents depending on the country. A per-message price is what stops automated sending from becoming spam, since software can push far more messages than a person ever would.
A template is a message Meta approves before you are allowed to send it. It is the only thing that can open a conversation with someone new or go out in bulk, and the two you plan for here are marketing and utility. Authentication is a third, used for one-time codes. A service message is an ordinary reply inside a conversation a customer already started: typed by an agent, written by your AI, or sent from your CRM. If it is not an approved template, it is a service message.
